Answer in three lines

  1. Different approaches? Yes. The Spanish note and the v2 report compute the tree-level Compton-like cross section γe→A′e (approach A). The v3 report uses in-medium γ→A′ oscillation (approach B, Danilov). B is the leading production channel; A is the secondary-photon correction (Demidov 2025). Park (and v2, NEON) compute only A.
  2. Different final formulas? Yes, at three levels: (i) the note's |M|², kinematics and range vs the exact ones; (ii) A vs B (×7.7 in TEXONO's 3–8 MeV window at the same ε); (iii) the detection factor 2/3 (Park, Lou–Wu) vs 1 (Danilov, v3); plus σ_tot = σ_KN (Park) vs XCOM total (Smirnov, NEON, Lou–Wu).
  3. From errors? (i) yes, algebraic, each with a one-line fix (report §6); (ii) omission of physics in Park's formula, not algebra; (iii) yes: 2/3 is the unpolarized average applied to a transverse beam. Du et al.'s ×10 is an error per Demidov 2025; consistent with my estimate.

The correct cross section

a = s−m² = 2mE,  b = u−m² = M²−2mx,  c = m²,  d = M²      (x = A′ lab energy, E = photon energy)
|M|²̄ = ε²e⁴ F,   F = −2(a/b + b/a) + 4(2c+d)[ (1/a+1/b) + c(1/a+1/b)² − d/(ab) ]
dσ/dx = ε²e⁴ F / (32π m E²),   x ∈ [x−, x+],  x± = [(E+m)(s+M²−m²) ± E√λ(s,m²,M²)]/(2s),  E_th = M + M²/2m

Verified by explicit 4×4 Dirac matrices + explicit polarization vectors at 60 random on-shell points: agrees with v2 eq (36), deNiverville eq (18–20), Smirnov eq (3) to 2×10⁻¹³ / 9×10⁻¹⁶. Ward identity numerically exact. d=0 → Klein–Nishina (sympy).
