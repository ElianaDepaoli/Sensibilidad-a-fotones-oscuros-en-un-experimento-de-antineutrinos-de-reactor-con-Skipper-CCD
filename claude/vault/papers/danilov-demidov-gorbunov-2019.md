# Danilov, Demidov, Gorbunov 2019 — Constraints on hidden photons produced in nuclear reactors
`papers_reactor_dark_photon/02_danilov_demidov_gorbunov_2019.pdf`, PRL 122, 041801.

**Claims.** Production is γ→X oscillation in the medium: P = ε² m_X⁴/[(Δm²)² + E²Γ²], m_γ ≈ 20 eV, 1/Γ ≈ 10 cm (eq 6); detection P = ε² m_X⁴/(Δm²)² (eq 9); no 2/3 (only transverse X produced); Park's numbers off by 3/2 at m_X ≳ 20 eV, insensitive below ~0.05 eV; TEXONO cap 195.7 one-sided, ×6 anti-Compton → 1174.1; NEOS analysis of Park rejected.
**Evidence.** Solution of the 2-state Hamiltonian; TEXONO published numbers.
**What we checked.** Their ε² limit gives 1.95e19 A'/s in 3–8 MeV at 1 GW, ε=1 (Park's spectrum) — 7.7× Park's Compton-like channel in the same window. Transverse-only detection factor 1 confirmed by explicit polarization calc. See [[oscillation-vs-compton-production]], [[two-thirds-detection-factor]].
