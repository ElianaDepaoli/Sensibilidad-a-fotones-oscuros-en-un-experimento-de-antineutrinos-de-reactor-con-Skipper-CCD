# Du, Liu, Wang, Wu 2024 — Amplifying nonresonant production of dark sector particles in scattering dominance regime
`papers_reactor_dark_photon/05_du_liu_wang_wu_2024.pdf`, PRD 109, 055041.

**Claims.** Compton rescattering of reactor photons enhances oscillation production by Γ/Γ_abs ~ 5–10 (MC: ×10); TEXONO ε² limit improves ~2–3×.
**What we checked.** Nothing rerun. Our single-scattering estimate of the Compton-like (secondary) channel is 13% of the direct channel in the 3–8 MeV window, consistent with [[demidov-gorbunov-polonski-2025]] (f = 1.1–1.2 there) and not with ×10. Demidov et al. identify the error: scattering treated as full collapse (missing 1/2, Γ_abs→Γ_tot). Status: superseded.
