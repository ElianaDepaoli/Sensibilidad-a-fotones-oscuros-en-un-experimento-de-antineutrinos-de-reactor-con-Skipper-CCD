# report_park_production_v2_en.pdf (apuntes/, 17 Sep 2026, ROOT/C++)
**Claims.** Exact tree-level F(a,b,c,d) eq 36, dσ/dx eq 38, primitive G(b) eq 41, exact range eq 12, threshold eq 8; its Fig 2 follows Park's Fig 1 (median residual 4% for M=0.1 MeV in 1.8–3.2 MeV); KN/NIST uranium table; no 2/3 in production.
**What we checked.** F confirmed to 2e-13 by explicit matrices and by two published forms; dG/db=F symbolically; threshold and range confirmed. Its fold (Fig 2) is NOT confirmed: Park's eq 1 evaluated with the same cross section gives a tail 4–8× below Park's Fig 1 / v2's Fig 2 above 2 MeV (normalisation of the fold checked to 0.1%). v2's code not available. Its physics scope (Compton-like only) omits the oscillation/direct channel — see [[oscillation-vs-compton-production]]. Its NIST numbers were recalled, not recomputed.
