# Park 2017 — Detecting dark photons with reactor neutrino experiments
`papers_reactor_dark_photon/01_park_2017.pdf`, PRL 119, 081801.

**Claims.** Reactor γ produce A' by γe→A'e (eq 1: dN/dE_A' = ∫ (1/σ_tot) dσ/dE_A' dN_γ/dE); σ_tot ≈ σ_C; eq 2: dσ/dE_A' ≈ ε² dσ_C/dE_r; detection eq 6 with a 2/3 factor; spectrum eq 3 = 0.58e18 (P/MW) e^{-E/0.91 MeV}; ε < 2.1e-5 (TEXONO), 1.3e-5 (NEOS).
**Evidence.** Fig 1 (A' spectra), his own unpublished generator.
**What we checked.** Photon integral above 1 MeV = 1.7588e20/s at 1 GW (he prints 1.76e20) ✓. His Fig 1 tail above 2 MeV equals the photon spectrum S_γ (0.065 vs 0.064 at 2 MeV, 0.007 vs 0.0072 at 4 MeV, 1e21/MeV/s), while his eq 1 with the exact cross section gives 0.017 and 0.0009: the figure is not eq 1. See [[oscillation-vs-compton-production]]. The 2/3 is the unpolarized average — wrong for the actual beam, see [[two-thirds-detection-factor]]. His production formula omits the dominant [[oscillation-vs-compton-production]] channel. σ_tot=σ_C overestimates the Compton-like yield 1.6–3.5× (uranium, per v2's NIST table).
