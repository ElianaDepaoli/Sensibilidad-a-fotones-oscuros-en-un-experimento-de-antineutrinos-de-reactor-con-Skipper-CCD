# Lou, Wu 2026 — Probing dark photons from nuclear de-excitation in reactor neutrino experiments
`papers_reactor_dark_photon/08_lou_wu_2026.pdf`, arXiv:2606.08487.

**Claims.** N*→N A' after neutron capture gives on-shell A' up to 6.9 MeV and beats the Compton-like channel; Compton-like flux eq 8 with XCOM σ_tot; inverse Compton-like eq 11 reduces to (2/3)ε² dσ_C (unpolarized average); App. A gives the CM |M|² for A'e→γe.
**What we checked.** Eq 11's 2/3 is the unpolarized average (confirmed) but the reactor beam is transverse, see [[two-thirds-detection-factor]]. Their de-excitation channel is the mass-basis face of Danilov's ε² oscillation term, see [[oscillation-vs-compton-production]]. App. A not checked.
