# Two production mechanisms for reactor A'
**B (leading):** in-medium γ→X oscillation, Danilov eq 6: P = ε² m⁴/[(Δm²)² + E²Γ²] → ε² for m_X ≫ m_γ, E_X = E_γ. In the mass basis this is direct A' emission at every photon-emitting vertex (Lou–Wu's de-excitation channel).
**A (subleading):** Compton-like γe→A'e, Park eq 1: ∫ S_γ (1/σ_tot) dσ/dx dE, A' downscattered. = Demidov 2025's secondary-photon term, f(E)−1 ≈ 0.1–0.2 at 3–4 MeV.
In TEXONO's 3–8 MeV window at 1 GW, ε=1, Park spectrum: A = 2.53e18/s, B = 1.95e19/s, ratio 0.13 (M-independent 0.1–1 MeV). Do not add A to B·f(E) — that double counts. σ_tot must be the XCOM total, not σ_KN. See [[dark-compton-cross-section]].

**Spectra** (`work/fig_dN_dE_two_models.pdf`, 1 GW, ε=1, M=0.1 MeV, 1e21/MeV/s): A = 0.108, 0.017, 0.0036, 0.0009 at 1, 2, 3, 4 MeV; B = S_γ = 0.193, 0.064, 0.021, 0.0072. Park's Fig 1 reads ≈0.40, 0.065, 0.022, 0.007 — it is B's tail, not A. Below 0.5 MeV A ≈ B.
