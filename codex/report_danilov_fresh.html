Fresh Danilov-guided TEXONO calculation

New code and new plots. No earlier project calculation or curve is imported. The numerical one-loop decay enhancement is published source data; the loop amplitude itself is not rederived.

The absorption-only plateau boundary is ε₉₅ = 1.3362703e-05 at 1 keV. This uses a one-sided Gaussian cap of 196.17227 selected events, transverse detection and assumed acceptance 1/6. It is a conditional reinterpretation, not a detector-calibrated exclusion.

Danilov’s source is coherent transverse conversion in an absorbing medium. It preserves the original photon energy. No Compton-convolved source is multiplied into it. Both Compton amplitudes are derived independently, including a new analytic longitudinal absorption trace. The transverse result has the ordinary Klein–Nishina massless limit.

Baseline decay losses are small at the boundary, but accepted detector decays are treated separately. The main result sets their acceptance to zero; a second curve assumes the same acceptance as absorption. Resonances and roots outside the declared small-mixing domain are omitted.

Published-normalization comparison remains unresolved: rescaling Park’s printed bound gives 2.9641217e-05; the direct source integral gives 1.3362703e-05. No normalization is fitted and the published figure is not claimed as reproduced.

Not established: actual energy-dependent signal response, material profiles, resonance transport, exact nuclear mass corrections and longitudinal nuclear emission, secondary reactor cascade, or the original TEXONO likelihood. At the upper mass endpoint the source’s relativistic expansion parameter can reach about 0.11 in the ROI.
