                                                        We calculate the exact width for a dark photon decaying to three photons at one loop order for dark
                                                      photon masses m0 below the e+ e− production threshold of 2me . We find substantial deviations from
                                                      previous results derived from the lowest order Euler-Heisenberg effective Lagrangian in the range
                                                      me . m0 ≤ 2me , where higher order terms in the derivative expansion are nonnegligible. This mass
                                                      range is precisely where the three photon decay takes place on cosmologically relevant timescales.
                                                      Our improved analysis opens a window for dark photons in the range 850 KeV . m0 ≤ 2me ,
