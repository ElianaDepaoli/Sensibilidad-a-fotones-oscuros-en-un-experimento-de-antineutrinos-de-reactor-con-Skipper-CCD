# ROOT figure and physics audit

Session-specific requirements are in [[darkphoton-v3-root/SKILL]]. Preserve graph
lifetimes, test plotted ranges, inspect PNG and PDF exports, verify source equation
coefficients, and distinguish source digitization from numerical reproduction.
No resonance limit without a justified finite detector propagation calculation.
Record source assumptions, selection efficiency, confidence convention, and all
generated figures in provenance. ROOT documentation consulted:
https://root.cern.ch/doc/master/classTGraph.html and
https://root-forum.cern.ch/t/no-display-when-drawing-graph/63217.
