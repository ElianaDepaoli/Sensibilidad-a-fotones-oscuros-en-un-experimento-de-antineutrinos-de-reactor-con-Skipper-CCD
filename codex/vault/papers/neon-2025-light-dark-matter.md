# NEON 2025 — light dark matter

Direct collaboration search using reactor-on minus reactor-off NaI(Tl) spectra. The signal is χ–electron scattering after invisible A′→χχ decay. It is not the same model as visible A′ scattering.

Local source: `papers_reactor_dark_photon/06_neon_2025.pdf`. Not independently reproduced.
