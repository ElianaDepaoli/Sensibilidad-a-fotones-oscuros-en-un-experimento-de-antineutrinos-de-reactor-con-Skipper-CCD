# Park 2017 — reactor dark photons

Claims γe→A′e production and A′e→γe detection. Recasts NEOS and TEXONO, reporting sub-MeV ε limits. The NEOS treatment and TEXONO acceptance were challenged by [[danilov-demidov-gorbunov-2019]].

Local sources: `papers_reactor_dark_photon/01_park_2017.pdf` and supplied journal PDF `papers_init/park2017.pdf`.

[[park-texono-reproduction]] now derives and checks production/detection cross sections in ROOT/C++. The printed TEXONO limit and complete Figure 1 remain unreproduced; failures and normalization diagnostics are documented. This run deliberately excludes later corrections.
