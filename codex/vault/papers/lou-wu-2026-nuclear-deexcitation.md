# Lou & Wu 2026 — nuclear de-excitation

Uses neutron-capture nuclear lines as on-shell A′ sources and recasts TEXONO scattering and three-hit pair-production data. Claims a 0.1–6.9 MeV mass reach, beyond conventional Compton-like production.
